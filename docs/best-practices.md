# Note-Taking Best Practices

## PARA (Projects, Areas, Resources, Archives)

- **Projects**: Active efforts with clear outcomes.
- **Areas**: Spheres of responsibility or interest.
- **Resources**: Useful information for future reference.
- **Archives**: Inactive items from the other three categories.

## Zettelkasten (Slip Box)

- **Atomic Notes**: Each note contains one idea.
- **Permanent Identifiers**: Notes are numbered or dated.
- **Links**: Connections between related notes.
- **Index**: A hub note to navigate the system.

## MOC (Map of Content) / Atomic

- **MOC**: A note that links to related atomic notes.
- **Atomic**: Small, self-contained notes with one idea each.

## Daily Notes

- **Date-Based**: One note per day.
- **Journaling**: Record thoughts, tasks, and events.
- **Linking**: Connect to other notes for context.

Choose the method that best fits your workflow and stick to it. Consistency is key to an effective note-taking system.
